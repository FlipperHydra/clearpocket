Two-minute tour

Plan, spend, adjust.

ClearPocket follows a simple zero-based workflow: only budget money you already have, and keep the remaining amount at zero. Unspent money carries forward to the next month.

  1. 1

    Add and assign income

    Record income as a transaction, then use “Assign money” or click a category amount.

  2. 2

    Record spending

    Add a transaction to reduce the category's available balance.

  3. 3

    Cover surprises

    Move money between categories without changing the total plan. Overspending reduces next month's ready-to-assign amount.
